About

At GCU, we understand academia is your top priority, but there's a wealth of knowledge to enjoy outside of the lecture halls. That's why we do our best to organize events that showcase the most enriching parts of the great state of Arizona and beyond.

  • Interact other students beyond your class roster.
  • Expand your horizons and see what you can learn outside of your textbook
  • Reset your brain and enjoy the various forms of recreation we have to offer.
