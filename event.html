Star Gazing Sponsored by GCU

  • Category: Nature
  • Date/Time: October 02 @8PM
  • Location: Glen Canyon

Intro: This October 2nd we would like to exhibit the excellent vantage points Glen Canyon has to offer when it comes to star gazing.

Description: The GCU events team will take students to the most popular vantage points offered by Glen Canyon when it comes to seeing the stars at night.

Attendees are expected to arrive at 8:00 P.M., and peripherals for gazing at the starts such as telescopes will be provided.

Schedule:

  • 8:00 P.M.: Students will meet with event staff at a GPS pin that will be provided after registration.
  • 8:15 P.M.: The event staff will guide students to the planned gazing site.
  • 8:30 P.M.: Star Gazing begins
  • 9:15 P.M.: S'mores
  • 10:30 P.M.: We will depart from the gazing site and dismiss the group.

Participants should direct any questions to our email at: glencanyonevents@GCU.edu

Date: October 2nd

Time: 8:00 P.M.

Location: Glen Canyon

Organizer: Dave Jonesy

Admission information: Admission is free for all enrolled students
